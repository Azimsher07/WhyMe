About WhyMe

Our mission and promise to you.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, judgment-free space for students to find clarity and support. We believe no student should have to carry the weight of the world alone. Mental health challenges are real, valid, and far more common than many realize.

WhyMe was created to bridge the gap between struggling in silence and seeking professional help. We offer a compassionate first step—a place to talk, be heard, and feel understood.

How it Works

WhyMe uses an empathetic AI companion powered by advanced natural language processing to listen and offer support. It is designed to be a comforting presence when you need to vent or seek advice.

Before you start chatting, you'll complete a brief questionnaire that helps the AI understand your current situation and provide personalized responses.

Important Disclaimer: WhyMe is an AI companion, not a replacement for professional therapy or medical advice. If you're experiencing a mental health crisis, please reach out to a qualified professional or call a crisis hotline immediately.

Privacy & Data

Your privacy is our top priority. Here's what you need to know:

  • Anonymous Storage:
    Chats are stored anonymously for quality improvement purposes.
  • No Personal Identifiers:
    No personal information (like your name, email, or IP address) is linked to your conversations.
  • Nobody Knows Who You Are:
    Your identity remains completely confidential.
  • Quality Improvement:
    Anonymized data may be reviewed by our team to improve the AI's responses and better serve students like you.

Why We Built This

Mental health challenges among students are at an all-time high. Many suffer in silence due to stigma, lack of resources, or not knowing where to turn. We built WhyMe to provide an accessible, immediate, and non-judgmental space for students to express themselves.

While WhyMe isn't a substitute for professional care, it can be a valuable first step in your mental health journey—helping you process your thoughts and pointing you toward additional resources when needed.
