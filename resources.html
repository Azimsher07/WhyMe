Calming Tools

Simple techniques to ground yourself.

  • Box Breathing: Inhale 4s, Hold 4s, Exhale 4s, Hold 4s. Repeat.
  • 5-4-3-2-1 Technique: Name 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
  • Progressive Relaxation: Tense and release each muscle group from toes to head.

Sleep Hygiene

Better sleep, better mental health.

  • Keep a consistent sleep schedule
  • Avoid screens 1 hour before bed
  • Limit caffeine after 2 PM
  • Create a dark, cool sleep environment
